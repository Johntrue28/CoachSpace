# 01 — PlayerPlus (TeamPlus / SpielerPlus): competitor analysis

> Researched on 6 November 2026 from public pages (links at the end). PlayerPlus's own web price list and
> "For Clubs" page could not be opened from the research session; the prices below are the App Store
> in-app purchase prices, which are exact for those stores.

## Summary

PlayerPlus was renamed **TeamPlus** outside the DACH region in May 2025 (app version 2.5.1) and stays
**SpielerPlus** in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. It is a freemium team-organisation app: free with
advertising, with a per-team Premium of about **€54 a year**.

1. **It is a product of a sports advertising company.** SPM Sportplatz Media GmbH (Hamburg) is first of all
   a marketer of amateur-football audiences (it markets Fussball.de for the German FA). The app builds
   audience; ads and Premium monetise it.
2. **Its core is very simple:** event → one-tap "going / not going" → the coach sees who is coming. Chat,
   team treasury, carpools and statistics all orbit that loop.
3. **The whole team uses it for free; one person pays** (usually the coach or the team kitty) to remove ads
   and lift limits. Prices are per team, not per player.
4. **Its moat is density in DACH:** 410,000 teams and about 5 million users according to its website. The app
   is already translated into Dutch and Spanish, but no local marketing or traction was found in the
   Netherlands or Spain.
5. **Exploitable weaknesses:** ads and advertising tracking (including precise location) in an app where about
   40% of teams are youth teams; little depth for the coach's training work; a young brand outside Germany.

## Business model

| Stream | Who pays | How |
|---|---|---|
| Advertising | Advertisers | Banners and full-screen ads in the free version. The App Store privacy label declares tracking with location and identifiers and precise location for third-party advertising |
| Team Premium | Coach, team manager or the team kitty | One payment per team unlocks Premium for **all** members of that team |
| Multi-team Premium | Clubs | Bundles of 2–25 teams at a discount; more than 25 on quote |
| Individual Premium | One user | Removes ads for that person only |
| Sponsored features | Brands | e.g. an in-team Bundesliga prediction game "powered by TEAMTIP" |

No payment processing fees (unlike Spond). The free tier must be genuinely good so the whole team adopts it;
the invitation is the growth channel. Limits sit where a team that already depends on the app feels them:
more than 30 members, roles, cancelling with notification, seeing when each member replied, no ads.

## What PlayerPlus does well (worth copying)

1. **The event is the unit of everything** — attendance, line-up, info, tasks and notices hang off an event.
2. **One-tap answer** from the notification or the list; the coach sees the count at once.
3. **Five-minute, four-step onboarding.**
4. **Growth by invitation**; parent accounts multiply users at no sales cost.
5. **Real-world roles** (coach, team manager, treasurer, player, parent).
6. **"Dressing-room" features** that create daily use (treasury, fines, carpools, polls).
7. **Pain-led messaging:** "no more chaotic chat histories or Excel sheets".
8. **Many sports and languages with one product** — the sport is a label, not a different app.

## Weaknesses and gaps

| Weakness | Evidence | How CoachSpace can use it |
|---|---|---|
| Ads in the free version | Reviews complain about full-screen ads | Free and **ad-free** for players and families, always |
| Advertising tracking in an app with many minors | Privacy label declares tracking incl. precise location; ~40% youth teams | "No ads, no tracking, data in the EU" for youth clubs and families |
| Performance | 2023 review: slow, chat disappearing and reappearing | Speed as a product requirement |
| Little depth for the coach | No library, plans or notes found | The coach's technical work is CoachSpace's natural space |
| Built for "the team", not the coach with many groups | Pricing and model per team | Product and price centred on the coach or school |
| Unclear web pricing | An external review could not read the prices; the Premium page showed an unfilled placeholder | Public, simple price |
| Young brand outside DACH | "TeamPlus" since May 2025; testimonials from German clubs | Local brand, language and support |
| Very German features | Fines catalogue, Bundesliga predictions | Adapt to each country's sports culture |

Honest caveat: PlayerPlus already ships Dutch and Spanish, and nothing above stops it from fixing these gaps.
CoachSpace's edge has to be local execution and the coach's method, not PlayerPlus being absent.

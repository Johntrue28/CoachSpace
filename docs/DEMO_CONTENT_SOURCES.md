# CoachSpace — Demo content sources

> Where the demo's fictional content and photographs come from. Trimmed on 7 November 2026; the older notes
> (search history, illustration candidates) are in Git history.

## Fictional content

All coaches, clubs, players, messages and schedules are invented. Drills and coaching points are written for
the demo from common coaching practice; no real person, club or brand appears. Never suggest that the people
in a stock photo are real CoachSpace coaches or customers.

`images.pexels.com` on the same day, looked at one by one, cropped to 16:10 and saved in two widths
(`public/photos/<name>-640.jpg` and `-1280.jpg`, JPEG quality 72, about 1.4 MB in all). They are used on
the welcome screen and Settings → Demo (covers), and Home's next-session card. All are under the **Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free
commercial use, credit not required, no selling unaltered copies, no implied endorsement by the people

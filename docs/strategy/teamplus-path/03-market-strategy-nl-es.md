# 03 — Market strategy: the PlayerPlus of the Netherlands and Spain

> Research of 6 November 2026. Recommendations are proposals for the owner; pricing changes replace D-012
> only after explicit approval.

## Thesis

Being "the PlayerPlus" of a country means being the app a team installs by default because the other teams
around it already use it. It is a game of local density, not of features. **Recommendation: start in Spain
(Balearic Islands) and treat the Netherlands as a second market and a validation lab.**

- **The Netherlands already has a local PlayerPlus, in Groningen:** Teamy (founded 2019/2020) says it connects
  more than 200,000 team players in the Netherlands and Belgium, took on an investor at the end of 2024 and
  has a club product ([Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/teamy-welcomes-investor-chris-stam/)). Spond,
  TeamPlus in Dutch and the KNVB's official apps are also there.
- **No dominant team app was found in Spain.** Visible Spanish tools (Clupik) target club administration; at
  team and family level WhatsApp is the incumbent.
- **The owner's unfair advantage is in Spain:** native language, a network in Mallorca (tennis schools already
  in the outreach campaign), knowledge of how sports schools work.
- **Size:** 4.38 million federated licences in Spain in 2025 ([CSD](https://www.educacionfpydeportes.gob.es/va/dam/jcr:502bb031-390e-4eb7-8152-866d1c9a9632/estadistica-de-deporte-federado-2025.pdf)), plus non-federated schools.

Competing as "another free team app" against Spond and Teamy is a distribution war. Competing as "the coach's
or school's tool that also brings players and families together" has fewer direct rivals.

## The Netherlands

Organised sport runs through the *vereniging* (volunteer-run association): about 22,000 associations
affiliated to NOC*NSF in 2022, down from 25,000 ten years earlier ([NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2475281)). Football
uses the KNVB/Sportlink system (Wedstrijdzaken and Voetbal.nl apps for the digital match form); basketball and
tennis have their own federation platforms. Teamy covers the PlayerPlus core (attendance, reminders, fixture
import, line-ups, tasks, chat, kitty) and now Teamy Clubs with volunteer rosters.

Openings: private coaches and schools that are not a *vereniging* (tennis/padel pros, private football
academies, student clubs); the coach's technical work; international and English-speaking communities.
**Conclusion:** a good place to validate the product in person while studying in Groningen; a poor place to
try to become the default team app now.

## Spain

Clubs with many age categories (*benjamín, alevín, infantil…*), monthly fees paid by families, weekly match
call-ups (*convocatorias*) and parents' WhatsApp groups. As a regional reference, the Valencian Community has
5,818 federated clubs ([Valencia News](https://valencianews.es/deportes/la-comunitat-valenciana-supera-las-420-000-licencias-federativas-y-consolida-el-crecimiento-del-deporte/)).
Competitors: Clupik (club administration, sold to boards), SportMember, Spond, TeamPlus and Teamy in Spanish
without signs of dominance, and local start-ups such as Esportter in Seville ([El Referente](https://elreferente.es/?p=18973)).

Openings: call-ups and attendance with families; **sports schools and academies** (tennis, padel, grassroots
football, basketball) with a coordinator, several coaches and many groups; privacy for minors as a selling
point. **Conclusion:** recommended first market, starting in Mallorca.

## Positioning and wedge

Position: **the app of the coach and the sports school, which also brings players and families together.**
Draft statement: "CoachSpace brings your groups together in one app: they know when training is, they tell
you if they're coming, and you have your exercises and plans at hand. No ads."

Wedge: tennis/padel schools and grassroots football schools in Mallorca with 2–6 coaches and 10–40 groups.
Buyer: the coordinator or director. Users: coaches (manage), players and families (answer and read).

## Business model and pricing hypotheses (to test, not decided)

| Plan | For | Includes | Price to test | Market reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Players, families, a coach with 1 group | Events, going/not going, change notices, messages, guardians; no ads | €0 | PlayerPlus, Spond, Teamy are free at this level |
| Coach Pro | Coach with several groups | Unlimited groups, library, plans, videos per player, notes, statistics | €6.99/month (€69/year) vs €12.99/month (€129/year) | 3 teams on PlayerPlus = €154.99/year |
| School | Coordinator with 2–15 coaches | Pro for every coach, coordinator view, shared library, roles | €29/month up to 5 coaches; €59/month up to 15 | Clupik from €35; 25 teams on PlayerPlus ≈ $760/year |
| Fee collection (later) | Schools collecting monthly fees | Card/SEPA payments | Per-payment fee below Spond | Spond: 2.5% + €0.20 |

Rules: never ads; charge for management and method, not for communicating; sell school plans on the web with
an invoice (store purchases carry store fees and rules).

## Go-to-market

The first 10 customers come in person, one by one. The first cold-email campaign got 0 replies from ~20.

Now: reply to the contact who uses PlayerPlus with three questions (what they use most, what annoys them, what
another app would need to make them switch); 5 short interviews with coaches using PlayerPlus, Teamy, Spond or
only WhatsApp; choose the final wedge.

Stages: founding coaches (0 → 10, free for the season in exchange for a 15-minute call every two weeks) →
invitation loop (10 → 100 groups) → first payments at the start of the next season (100 → 300 groups) →
scalable channels (Spanish App Store search, short coach content, municipal schools, regional federations,
referrals).

| Metric | Measures | Starting signal (to adjust) |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly active groups | Real use | Grows every week for 8 weeks |
| % of invited members who answer | Whether the PlayerPlus loop works | Above 60% |
| Invitations accepted per group | Viral growth | Above 70% of the group joins |
| Coaches back 4 weeks in a row | Retention | 4 of 5 founders |
| Coaches paying full price | Willingness to pay | At least 3 before building in-app payments |

## Risks and legal requirements

| Risk | Why it matters | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Nobody switches | Switching means convincing ~20 people | Free tier at least as good; sign-up under a minute; start with teams on no app |
| Minors' data (GDPR) | Many groups are children | Guardian accounts; minors never sign up alone; minimal data; EU hosting |
| Digital consent age | Spain: 14 today, a bill raises it to 16 ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/es/insights/espana-busca-proteger-los-menores-en-el-entorno-digital-claves-del-proyecto-de-ley)); Netherlands: 16 | Design for 16 in both; check the law's status before launch |
| Better-funded competitors | They can copy features | Win on local presence and the coach's method |
| Two countries at once | Double language, marketing, support | One main market (Spain); NL for validation |
| Founder time | First year at university | Short stages with exit criteria |

Not legal advice: consult a professional before launching with real data.
